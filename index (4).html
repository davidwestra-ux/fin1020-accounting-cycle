The Accounting Cycle

A structured pathway from source documents and transaction analysis through journalizing, posting, and preparation of a trial balance.

Learning pathway

  • 1. Source Documents
    Purpose, evidence, and business records    →
  • 2. Transaction Analysis
    Accounts affected and debit/credit reasoning    →
  • 3. Journalizing
    Record transactions in the general journal    →
  • 4. Posting
    Transfer entries to ledger accounts    →
  • 5. Trial Balance
    Test debit and credit equality    →
